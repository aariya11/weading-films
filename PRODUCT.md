## Platform

## Users

Primary clients are luxury couples, discerning families, and wedding organizers across Bhubaneswar, Odisha, and international destination circuits. They seek high-end narrative wedding cinema, sacred Vedic ceremony documentation, and archival fine-art keepsakes for multi-day celebrations. Their situation is high-stakes, deeply emotional, and once-in-a-lifetime; they require uncompromising reliability, emotional presence, and white-glove concierge attention.

## Product Purpose

WEDDING FILMS exists to immortalize sacred matrimonial devotion into cinema-grade visual heirlooms. Success means delivering an editorial-grade, emotionally resonant digital and physical archive (4K master features, theatrical trailers, negative suites, Italian leather monographs) that preserves both personal family bonds and timeless Odia cultural heritage across generations.

## Positioning

Cinematic film-school narrative direction (35mm optical grain, prime cinema lenses, DaVinci Resolve color grading) rooted in Odia temple heritage and bespoke archival craftsmanship. Unlike conventional high-volume event videography or generic social-reel vendors, WEDDING FILMS limits commissions to ensure single-wedding unit exclusivity, cinematic narrative depth, and museum-grade physical delivery.

## Operating Context

- **Client Evaluation & Commissioning**: Couples and families browse curated portfolio films, review comprehensive commission tiers, settle production retainers via verified bank escrow (UPI, Card, NetBanking), and receive instant counter-signed agreements.
- **Production Rituals**: Multi-day ceremonial coverage ranging from intimate Haldi and bridal temple walks to high-energy Baraat processions, Sangeet choreography, and Vedic Agni Kunda rites.
- **Post-Production & Archival Delivery**: Raw camera negative preservation on 4TB SSDs, full-resolution curated photo negatives, private VIP digital screening suites, and handcrafted Italian Tuscan leather albums.
